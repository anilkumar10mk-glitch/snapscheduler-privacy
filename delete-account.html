SnapScheduler Account and Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your SnapScheduler account and associated data, please send an email to:

aks.apps10@gmail.com

Include the Google account email address used to sign in to SnapScheduler.

What will be deleted

  • Your SnapScheduler account
  • Stored scheduling preferences
  • Associated screenshot and app data stored by SnapScheduler services

Retention

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Some records may be retained temporarily if required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
