Privacy Policy for SnapScheduler

Effective date: 12 August 2026

SnapScheduler is a screenshot scheduling utility that allows users to capture screenshots of web pages and optionally send them through services configured by the user.

Information We Collect

SnapScheduler may collect or process:

  • Website URLs entered by the user
  • Screenshot images generated by the app
  • Scheduling preferences created by the user
  • Basic app usage information required for app functionality

How We Use Information

The information is used only to:

  • Capture screenshots requested by the user
  • Execute scheduled screenshot tasks
  • Deliver screenshots to destinations configured by the user (such as Telegram or email, if enabled)

Data Storage

User-created schedules and settings may be stored locally on the device or in services chosen by the user. We do not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

SnapScheduler may interact with third-party services selected by the user, such as Telegram or email providers, solely to deliver screenshots or notifications requested by the user.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data. Information is shared only with third-party services that the user explicitly configures for screenshot delivery.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

SnapScheduler is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same URL.
